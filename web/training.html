Symbol types

Each legend symbol learns from its own reviews. Approve correct matches and reject wrong ones from a whole-set scan, then press Train: the next whole-set scan throws out matches that look like that symbol's rejected ones. A symbol's learning is switched on only after it proves itself on reviews it was not trained on (it must not lose correct matches). Pins added by hand are counted but not learned from yet.
